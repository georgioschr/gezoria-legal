Privacy policy

DishKeeper
Publisher: GEZORIA (developer brand)
Effective 3 November 2026 · version 1.0
Privacy contact: gezoriainfo@gmail.com

Your private collection

Recipe links, titles, tags, collections, notes, favorites, photos, ingredients, user-added source domains and recipe-opening counts are stored in private app storage on your device. GEZORIA does not receive your collection. There is no account, public feed, developer-hosted collection database, advertising SDK, analytics SDK or cloud AI in this version.

Websites and previews

Automatic previews contact the linked website and public image hosts. Those providers receive your IP address, requested address and ordinary network request information. Requests do not use your social-app login session. You can disable automatic previews in Settings; in-progress requests may finish, and manual refresh still contacts the source. Opening an original recipe uses another app or browser, under that provider’s privacy policy. A saved link may contain identifiers in its address.

Choices, backups and deletion

You can edit or delete recipes, disable previews, and delete all collection data in Settings. Local collection data remains until you delete it, clear app storage or uninstall. Backups are created only at your request in the location you choose. Encrypted backups require your password; GEZORIA cannot recover it. Readable JSON exports contain saved information and photos without password protection. Files in a cloud folder are handled by your chosen storage provider. Exported files remain until you delete them separately. Android system pickers grant access to the specific files and photos you select; the app does not request broad photo-library access.

Local suggestions and security

Suggestions use saved text, collections, favorites and opening counts on your device. They do not analyze videos or send content to AI services. Photo cropping happens on your device; DishKeeper does not automatically capture other apps. Optional app lock uses Android’s device credential confirmation; the app does not receive your device PIN or password. App lock does not add separate encryption to local app files. Supported-source restrictions are on by default. You can add sites or disable restrictions after a warning. Link checks are not a malware scan or a guarantee of safe, legal or cooking-only content.

Support information

If you email gezoriainfo@gmail.com, GEZORIA receives your email address and the information you send. Gmail processes these communications under Google’s applicable policies. Support information is used to answer and troubleshoot requests, based on the legitimate interest in providing support and protecting the service. Do not send passwords or private backups. Support correspondence is retained for up to 12 months after resolution, unless a longer period is needed for a legal obligation or dispute; unnecessary copies are deleted sooner. We do not sell personal information.

Your privacy rights

Where applicable law provides these rights, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to processing of support information by emailing the contact above. We may need proportionate information to verify a request. GEZORIA cannot access or remotely delete your device collection; use the in-app controls. You may complain to your local data-protection authority, including the Cyprus Commissioner for Personal Data Protection at dataprotection.gov.cy. This does not limit other rights available under applicable law.

Children and changes

DishKeeper is a general-audience recipe organizer and is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly solicit children’s personal information through support. Contact us if you believe a child has sent personal information. Material changes to data practices will be reflected in an updated policy and, where needed, additional notice or choices before the new processing begins.
